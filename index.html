Capabilities

Two core strengths. One unified partner.

From initial 3D parametric CAD modeling and DFM analysis to multi-axis CNC machining, assembly, and CMM metrology.

Pillar 01

Mechanical CAD & Engineering Design

Complete design lifecycle support. We convert rough concepts, legacy 2D drawings, or physical samples into verified, production-ready 3D parametric models and manufacturing prints.

  • 3D CAD Modeling & Assemblies — Fusion 360, SolidWorks, CATIA
  • 2D Manufacturing Drawings — Strict GD&T, datum structures, BS 8888 / ISO standards
  • Design for Manufacture (DFM) — Geometry optimization, tool clearance, cost reduction
  • Reverse Engineering — Physical components and 3D point clouds converted to CAD
  • Tooling & SPM Design — Checking fixtures, welding jigs, progressive press dies
Pillar 02

Precision CNC Manufacturing

Comprehensive machining capabilities across multi-axis VMC milling, high-precision turning, precision grinding, and sheet metal fabrication.

  • Multi-Axis VMC Milling — Envelopes up to 1250 × 500 × 500 mm
  • CNC Turning & Turn-Mill — Diameters from Ø5 mm up to Ø500 mm
  • Precision Surface Grinding — Flatness & parallelism within 0.003 mm
  • Materials — Aluminium (6082/7075), Stainless (304/316), EN19/42CrMo4, Tool Steels (D2/O1), Bronze, Engineering Plastics
  • Surface Finishes — Hard Anodising, Black Oxide, Passivation, Plating, Heat Treatment (up to 62 HRC)
