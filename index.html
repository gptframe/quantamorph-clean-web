STAGE 0 // RAW ENGINEERING BILLET
01 // RAW STOCK

Certified Raw Material

The manufacturing process begins with solid, ultrasound-tested rectangular billet stock in aerospace aluminium, stainless steel, or hardened alloy.

  • Material GradeAluminium 6082-T6 / EN19 Steel
  • Initial StateSolid Rectangular Billet
  • Traceability100% Mill Test Certification
02 // ROUGHING & DRILLING

High-Feed CNC Roughing

High-power CNC milling centers evacuate bulk material, plunge primary drilling patterns, and machine deep internal pocket channels.

  • OperationHigh-Feed Pocketing & Plunge Drilling
  • ToolingCarbide Multi-Flute Roughing Endmills
  • Stock RemovalUp to 70% bulk material evacuated
03 // FINISHING & GD&T

Precision 5-Axis Finishing

High-speed finish passes generate fine-pitch tapped threads, chamfered edge details, counterbores, and mirror surface finishes down to Ra 0.4 µm.

  • TolerancesDown to ±0.005 mm
  • Feature DetailingBores, Keyways, Chamfers & Taps
  • Surface TexturePrecision Milled / Ground Surface
04 // QUALITY & METROLOGY

Multi-Axis CMM Inspection

Every critical datum, hole concentricity, and surface profile is verified on coordinate measuring machines and digital height gauges before sign-off.

  • Metrology RigCMM & Mitutoyo Digital Micrometers
  • Verification100% GD&T True Position & Runout
  • DeliverableSigned Dimensional Inspection Report (DIR)
05 // PACKING & DISPATCH

Corrosion-Protected Delivery

Components are ultrasonic cleaned, treated with VCI anti-corrosion protection, laser-etched with part numbers, and packed in secure custom foam for direct delivery.

  • ProtectionVCI Anti-Corrosion Wrap & Foam Clamping
  • IdentificationLaser Part Number & Batch QR Marking
  • DeliveryDirect to Client Assembly Lines
